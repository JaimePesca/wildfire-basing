Dear Guest Editors,

Please consider our manuscript "The Cycle-Constrained Aerial
Suppression Base and Water Point Location Problem: Joint Siting under
Uncertainty with an Application to Cundinamarca, Colombia" for the
International Transactions in Operational Research special issue on
Operations Research in Wildfire Management.

The paper addresses two of the special issue's listed topics, resource
location-allocation in wildfire preparedness and combined problems, by
introducing, to our knowledge, the first model that sites aerial
suppression bases and water refill points jointly, with aircraft
productivity endogenous to the base-fire-water refill cycle, as a
two-stage mean-risk (CVaR) stochastic program. A fix-and-optimize
matheuristic with provably exact reduced subproblems makes the full
candidate catalog tractable, and a fully open-data case study for
Cundinamarca, Colombia, is built around the country's real Sikorsky
S-70i Firehawk acquisition, including an SAA replication analysis with
an optimality-gap estimate and an operational reading of the plan in
real places.

The manuscript is original, unpublished, and not under consideration
elsewhere. All data sources are open and every experiment is
reproducible from the companion repository, which we will make
available to reviewers.
