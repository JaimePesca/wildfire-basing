Reading Table~\ref{tab:gap} by column isolates the gap. Cycle-dependent aircraft
productivity (Cyclic) appears only at the operational level, where the
infrastructure is given and no siting takes place; the joint siting of bases and
water refill points (Joint) appears in no prior model; and no work combines
cycle-dependent productivity with strategic, uncertainty-aware siting. Since
columns Joint, Cyclic and Strategic are exactly conditions (a), (b) and (c) of
the gap statement, the table and the verbal argument stand or fall together. The
proposed model is the only row that satisfies all three, under stochastic
programming with a mean-risk CVaR objective. The nearest works each own a single
one of these pillars and none of the others: \citet{maras2023} site both bases
and water but in two sequential deterministic models, without a cycle;
\citet{rodriguezbarreiro2026} capture the cycle, and even select loading points,
but operationally, with given infrastructure and no uncertainty; and
\citet{huang2025} are strategic and stochastic but decide no water point and use
a coverage radius rather than a refill cycle.
